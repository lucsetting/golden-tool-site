Golden Tool is operated by Nevian Enterprise LLC ("we", "us"), a limited liability company registered in Wyoming, United States, with its principal office at 525 Randall Ave, Ste 100-865, Cheyenne, WY 82001, United States. We are the company responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: office@nevian.io. Full company details are in our Imprint.

This policy explains what we collect when you visit this site or use the Golden Tool app, and how we handle it.

2. What we collect

Account. Your email address, used to sign you in (one-time code by email, then an optional password) and to tie your data to your account.

App data you enter. Your monthly limit, expenses, categories, recurring items, goals, debts and settings. This data is stored so that it syncs between your devices. We do not connect to your bank and we never see card or account numbers.

Purchases. Our payment provider sends us the purchase event (your email, the product, the order ID and its status) so the add-ons you bought unlock in your account and refunds revoke them. It processes your payment details under its own privacy policy.

Technical data. Our hosting and infrastructure providers log standard request data (IP address, browser, time) to run the service securely. This site uses no advertising pixels and no analytics cookies.

3. How we use it

  • To sign you in, keep your account secure and sync your data across devices.
  • To unlock what you purchased and honor refunds.
  • To answer support requests.
  • To send transactional emails only: sign-in codes and purchase-related messages. We do not send marketing email without your consent.

4. Emails and how to unsubscribe

We send two kinds of email. Service emails, such as sign-in codes and messages about your order, are needed to run your account. Newsletters and other marketing emails are sent only if you asked to receive them.

To stop receiving newsletters or marketing emails from us, use either of these:

  • Click the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any newsletter or marketing email we send. It takes effect right away.
  • Write to office@nevian.io from the address you want removed, with "Unsubscribe" in the subject line. We remove the address within 10 business days.

Unsubscribing is free and does not affect your purchase or your access to Golden Tool. Service emails that you trigger yourself, such as a sign-in code, are still sent, because the app cannot work without them. To close your account and stop every email, ask us to delete your data as described under "Your rights".

5. Storage, cookies and local data

The app keeps a copy of your data in your browser's local storage so it works offline, and stores a session token to keep you signed in. Clearing site data on a device removes that copy; your account data remains on our servers until you delete it.

6. Service providers

  • Supabase (database, authentication and email codes), servers located in the United States.
  • Payment provider (checkout, payment, invoicing, refunds).
  • GitHub or a comparable static host (delivery of the website and app files).

Each provider processes data only to deliver its part of the service.

7. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Account data is isolated per user with database-level access rules, so one account cannot read another. No method is perfectly secure; keep your password private.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your account data while the account exists. You can erase all app data from Settings → Data at any time, and you can request deletion of the account itself by emailing office@nevian.io. Purchase records are kept as long as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Email us and we will act on the request within 60 days. EU and UK residents may also complain to their local data-protection authority.

10. Children

Golden Tool is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Why it exists

We wanted one number, and nothing else wanted to give it to us.

We built Golden Tool for ourselves first. Every budgeting app we tried kept getting heavier: bank logins, subscription tiers, notifications about notifications. What we actually opened the app for was a single question: how much can I spend today and still be fine at the end of the month?

So we made a tool that answers that question and stays out of the way. You set a limit, you write down what you spend, and the dashboard does the arithmetic. That's the core of the product, and we intend to keep it that way. Budgets, bills and the rest are there when you want them.

Questions

Before you decide

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Golden Tool runs in the browser on any phone or computer. Open it in Safari or Chrome, sign in, and your data is the same on every device.

Do I need to install anything?

No. You can use it straight from the link. If you want it on your home screen, open the link on your phone and choose Share, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens like a regular app.

Is my data private?

Your expenses are stored in your own account, protected by your email and password. Golden Tool does not connect to your bank and has no access to your accounts. You enter what you choose to enter. If you import a statement file from your bank, it is read in your browser, and only the rows you choose are saved to your account.

What if I skip a day?

Nothing breaks. Add the expenses when you remember, with the right date, and the pace and "safe to spend" update. The tool is built for real months, not perfect ones.

What are the add-ons?

Three optional extras inside the app: Golden Freedom (debt payoff plan, what-if and payment log), Golden Goals (savings goals, automatic plan and challenges) and Golden Vision (trends, net worth and yearly report). Category budgets, bills and cash flow are part of the core tool. The core tool works without them. Lifetime Access can be added at checkout so your access doesn't end after 12 months.

How do refunds work?

Within 60 days of purchase you can request a refund by emailing office@nevian.io, and you'll get the full amount back.

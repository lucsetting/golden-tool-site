Questions

Before you decide

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Golden Tool runs in the browser on any phone or computer. Open it in Safari or Chrome, sign in, and your data is the same on every device.

Do I need to install anything?

No. You can use it straight from the link. If you want it on your home screen, open the link on your phone and choose Share, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens like a regular app.

Is my data private?

Your expenses are stored in your own account, protected by your email and password. Golden Tool does not connect to your bank and has no access to your accounts. You enter what you choose to enter.

What if I skip a day?

Nothing breaks. Add the expenses when you remember, with the right date, and the pace and "safe to spend" update. The tool is built for real months, not perfect ones.

What are the add-ons?

Three optional extras inside the app: Debt Payoff Planner, Savings Goals, and Reports & Budgets. The core tool works without them. Lifetime Access can be added at checkout so your access doesn't end after 12 months.
